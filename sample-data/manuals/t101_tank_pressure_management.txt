TITLE: LNG Storage Tank T-101 Vapor Space Pressure Management
EQUIPMENT_ID: T-101
EQUIPMENT_NAME: LNG Storage Tank T-101
DOC_TYPE: manual

This guide covers vapor-space pressure readings on LNG storage tank T-101. Normal operating range is 8-22 kPag. Brief excursions above this range, typically lasting one to a few hours, are expected during scheduled boil-off gas venting operations, planned tank pressure-relief cycles, or immediately following a large LNG receipt that displaces vapor space. These transient, short-duration bumps that return to the normal range on their own, especially when they coincide with a known or scheduled venting/receipt operation, are typically benign and can be logged and dismissed after confirming the operational context.

A pressure reading that continues climbing rather than returning to baseline within a few hours, or that approaches the 30 kPag critical threshold, is a different and more serious situation potentially indicating a relief system issue, vapor return line blockage, or an unplanned heat-load increase, and warrants immediate operator attention regardless of whether a scheduled operation was in progress.

Recommended response for an above-normal pressure reading:
1. Check the operations log for a scheduled venting operation, relief cycle, or recent LNG receipt coinciding with the pressure rise.
2. If a coincident scheduled operation is confirmed and the pressure trend shows signs of returning toward baseline, this is very likely a benign, expected transient and can be logged and dismissed as such.
3. If no coincident scheduled operation is found, or if the pressure continues rising rather than returning toward baseline, escalate immediately per the tank pressure high-pressure response procedure and do not wait for the critical threshold to be reached.
4. Any pressure excursion, benign or not, should still be logged in the tank's pressure history for trending purposes.

This is decision-support guidance only. A qualified operator or reliability engineer must confirm root cause and operational context from current field data before taking any action; nothing in this document authorizes automatic equipment control.
